Privacy Policy — HK Mixed Keyboard

私隱政策 — HK Mixed Keyboard

Last updated: 2026-06-16

English

HK Mixed Keyboard ("the app") is an Android input method (keyboard). We take

your privacy seriously. This policy explains what the app does and does not do

with your data.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no

internet access and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

What the app processes

  • Text you type. As an input method, the app necessarily processes the keystrokes you enter in order to produce candidate characters and words. This processing happens entirely on your device, in memory. Typed text is never sent off the device.
  • Personal dictionary / learned words. To improve suggestions, the app may store words you add or frequently use in a local database on your device only. This data never leaves your device. You can view, edit, export, and delete it at any time from the app's settings ("管理自訂詞語", "匯出 / 匯入詞庫", "清除所有個人詞庫").
  • Settings. Your preferences (input mode, vibration, sound, etc.) are stored locally on your device.

Sensitive fields

When you type into a password or other sensitive field, the app enters a "safe

mode": it shows no candidates and does not learn from or store that input.

Data sharing and network access

  • The app requests no internet permission and makes **no network connections**. No data is uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Cloud backup is disabled (`allowBackup="false"`), so your personal dictionary is not copied to cloud backup services.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE — used only for optional key-press haptic feedback.

Data you control

If you use the export feature, a CSV file containing your custom words is written

to a location you choose. Anything you do with that exported file afterwards is

under your control.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Open-source data

The app bundles dictionary and frequency data derived from open-source projects;

see the in-app "開源授權與資料來源" screen (and the `NOTICE` file) for attributions.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last

updated" date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: mehlw2011@gmail.com

繁體中文

HK Mixed Keyboard（「本應用程式」）是一款 Android 輸入法（鍵盤）。我們非常重視

您的私隱。本政策說明本應用程式如何處理您的資料。

摘要

本應用程式不會收集、傳送或分享任何個人資料。 它沒有網絡存取權限，亦不含任何

分析、廣告或追蹤程式碼。

本應用程式處理的內容

  • 您輸入的文字。 作為輸入法，本應用程式必須處理您輸入的按鍵，以產生候選字詞。 所有處理只在您的裝置上、於記憶體中進行，輸入文字絕不會離開您的裝置。
  • 個人詞庫 / 學習詞語。 為改善建議，本應用程式可能將您新增或常用的詞語儲存於 裝置本機的資料庫中，此資料絕不會離開您的裝置。您可隨時於設定中檢視、編輯、匯出 及刪除（「管理自訂詞語」、「匯出 / 匯入詞庫」、「清除所有個人詞庫」）。
  • 設定。 您的偏好設定（輸入模式、震動、聲音等）只儲存於裝置本機。

敏感欄位

當您在密碼或其他敏感欄位輸入時，本應用程式會進入「安全模式」：不顯示候選字，亦

不會學習或儲存該輸入。

資料分享與網絡存取

  • 本應用程式不要求網絡權限，亦不會建立任何網絡連線。沒有任何資料會上傳給 我們或任何第三方。
  • 本應用程式不含廣告或第三方分析 SDK。
  • 已停用雲端備份（`allowBackup="false"`），您的個人詞庫不會複製到雲端備份服務。

權限

  • 震動 (VIBRATE) — 僅用於可選的按鍵震動回饋。

您可控制的資料

若您使用匯出功能，包含自訂詞語的 CSV 檔案會寫入您所選擇的位置。之後您對該檔案的

任何處置皆由您自行控制。

兒童私隱

本應用程式不會在知情下收集任何人（包括兒童）的資料。

開源資料

本應用程式內置衍生自開源項目的詞庫與字頻資料；署名詳見應用程式內「開源授權與

資料來源」畫面（及 `NOTICE` 檔案）。

變更

我們可能更新本政策。重大變更會以上方「最後更新」日期反映。
