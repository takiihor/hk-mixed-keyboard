HK Mixed Keyboard — Open Source Notices / 開源授權與資料來源

third-party open-source projects listed below. Each project's data remains
under its own license. We gratefully acknowledge these sources.

本應用程式內置的輸入碼、詞庫及字頻資料，衍生自下列開源項目。各項資料
均依其原有授權條款發佈。謹此致謝。

Modifications
-------------
The above data sets were filtered, re-ranked by frequency, reformatted to CSV,
normalized to toneless continuous Pinyin keys where applicable, and in some
cases supplemented with curated entries. The derived dictionary
files distributed with this app remain subject to the licenses above, including
their attribution and (where applicable) share-alike / open-database terms.

Local license texts are bundled beside this notice:
GPL-3.0.txt, CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt, CC-BY-4.0.txt, ODbL-1.0.txt, and Apache-2.0.txt.
The derived CSV database files are included in the app under assets/corpus/
and in the source repository under android/app/src/main/assets/corpus/.

衍生詞庫檔案經篩選、重新排序、轉為 CSV 格式並補充部分人工條目，仍受上述
授權條款約束（包括署名及相應的相同方式分享 / 開放資料庫條款）。
授權全文與此通知一同內置；衍生 CSV 詞庫位於 assets/corpus/。
